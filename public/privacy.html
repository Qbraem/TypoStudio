StudioTypo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: Studio Typo does not collect, store or sell your personal data. Your designs and photos stay on your device.

1. Who we are

Studio Typo ("the app", "we", "us") is an app for creating typography stickers for social media. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use the iOS app or the web version at studio-typo.onrender.com.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • Photos you load as a background are only displayed on your device. They are never uploaded to us.

Location and weather (Live designs)

Live designs can show your city and the current weather. This is optional: we only ask for your location when you tap Use my location, and you can always type a city and temperature yourself instead. When you allow it, your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) is sent directly from your device to Open-Meteo (weather) and BigDataCloud (city name) to look up the result. We do not receive, store or share your location. The city name and temperature are kept only on your device so the design can show them, and you can change or remove them at any time.

3. Information stored on your device

Your saved designs, favourites and settings are stored locally on your device (and, on iOS, in your private iCloud storage if you have iCloud enabled). We cannot access this data. Deleting the app removes the local data.

4. Saving and sharing images

When you save a design to Photos or share it (for example to a social app), the image is passed directly to the app or service you choose. On iOS, the app only asks for permission to add images to your photo library; it does not read your library.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We do not receive or store your payment details. To check whether you have Studio Typo Pro, the app receives an anonymous purchase status from Apple and from our purchase-management provider RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app-specific identifier and your purchase history only.

6. Web version and hosting

The web version is hosted by Render. Like any web server, it may temporarily keep technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify you. All fonts are included in the app itself; no third-party font service is used.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have any question about your privacy or want to exercise your rights under the GDPR, please contact us using the address below.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
