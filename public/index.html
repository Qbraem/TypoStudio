HOW TO

From Studio Typo
to your Story

Four quick steps to put your typography on top of any Instagram Story.

  1. 1
    Pick & style your designChoose a design in the Library and make it yours in Edit: text, font, colours.
  2. 2
    Save it to your Camera RollTap Save, then choose Save Image. The PNG is transparent: only the letters, no background.
    Save Image
    Copy
  3. 3
    Open your photo in StoriesIn Instagram, swipe right or tap + → Story and pick the photo you want to post.
  4. 4
    Add the PNG as a photo stickerTap the sticker icon , choose Photo and select your PNG. Pinch to resize, drag to place, and share!
    Slow
    Mornings
Quick tipYou can also tap Copy in step 2, then open your Story and tap Add sticker when Instagram offers to paste it.
