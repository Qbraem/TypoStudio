## Description (4000)
Make your social Stories stand out with beautiful typography.

Studio Typo gives you 180+ ready-made text designs for every mood and moment – from slow coffee mornings and weekend brunches to travel diaries, cosy nights, quotes and seasonal holidays. Pick a design, make it yours and save it as a transparent PNG to place on top of any photo.

EVERY TEMPLATE IS EDITABLE
• Tap any text to write your own words
• 50+ hand-picked fonts: elegant scripts, bold retro letters, editorial serifs and handwriting
• Change colours, size, spacing, rotation and arcs
• Drag, pinch and rotate right on your photo

LIVE DESIGNS
• Designs that fill in today's day, date, time, temperature, weather and your city
• Location is optional: type it in yourself or let the app look it up once

LITTLE DETAILS, BIG DIFFERENCE
• Ornaments: sparkles, hearts, drops, stars, swashes, coffee beans and more
• Travel & holiday icons: suitcase, plane, ticket, snowflake, tree, turkey…
• Print texture, knock-out effects and multicolour letters
• Paragraph blocks for quotes, notes, recipes and captions

SEE IT BEFORE YOU POST
• Preview your design on your own photos with the Story layout on top
• Save a crisp, transparent PNG straight to your Photos
• Share it directly to your favourite social apps
