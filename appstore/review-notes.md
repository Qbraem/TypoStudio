Sign-in required: No (no account, no login).

Thank you for reviewing Studio Typo.

Studio Typo is a typography editor for social stories. Users pick a title design, edit text, font and colour on top of a preview photo, and save the result as a transparent PNG sticker to Photos or share it.

HOW TO TEST
1. Open the app → tap any design in the Library.
2. Tap "Edit" to change text, font, size, style and colour.
3. Tap "Save" → the PNG is saved to Photos (permission: add-only) or shared via the share sheet.

IN-APP PURCHASES (Studio Typo Pro)
- Designs marked with a lock are Pro. Tap one, or the crown button at the top, to open the paywall.
- Products: "studiotypo_pro_yearly" (auto-renewable yearly subscription, 7-day free trial) and "studiotypo_pro_lifetime" (non-consumable, one-time).
- "Restore Purchases", Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links are at the bottom of the paywall.
- The free version stays fully usable with the free designs.

LOCATION (optional)
- Only used by the "Live" designs (e.g. "currently in Antwerpen · 15°"). Open the "Live" category and tap the location pill; the system permission prompt appears.
- Coarse, when-in-use only. Coordinates are rounded and only used to look up the city name and the current weather. Nothing is stored on a server or linked to the user.
- Users can decline and type a city and temperature manually instead.

DEVICES
- Supports iPhone, iPad and the iPhone Duo (inner and outer display), portrait and landscape.
